Museum Toolbox.

Downloadable planning templates, worksheets, and checklists for every phase of museum development.

The Museum Toolbox is the working companion to Museums 101: the documents behind the book, in editable Word and Excel formats, so you can adapt them for your own museum, board, or class.

How to get access.

  1. Find the password in Museums 101, page 49 (Chapter 8: Collections).
  2. Email it along with your name and institution, and I'll send you the Toolbox.
