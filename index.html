Museum Vitality Index

A better way to measure institutions.

A 0–100 scoring framework that benchmarks museums of every size using size-normalized metrics drawn from IRS Form 990 data, visitor reviews, digital analytics, and community context. A $500K museum can outrank a $25M institution if it's running well. Every metric is a ratio, never a raw number.
